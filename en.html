From the Crystal Valley into the whole world

For decades, we have brought new ideas to glassmaking – combining colors with crystal and glass components. We melt 40 tons of glass every day. Our passion and an unbroken tradition dating back to 16th-century master glassmakers helped us grow into one of the world's leading glass companies.

People in more than 140 countries admire our craft. We are present from the USA to China. We are the only company in the world to offer the smallest machine-cut synthetic stone – only 0.5 mm in size.
